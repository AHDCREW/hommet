## Part A — Reference-informed direction

### 1. Reference review

All six reference URLs were opened for this review. Observations below concern retrieved page content, navigation and link destinations; this was not a pixel-level visual, responsive or performance audit. Search-provider crawl freshness varies, particularly for IMKAT. Visual specifications below are proposed interpretations of Hommet's brief.

#### Cosentino — primary structural reference

**Observed:** The parent site combines brands, spaces, inspiration, showrooms, professional audiences and corporate information. Its homepage connects application areas, product browsing and project inspiration within the parent identity. [Source: Cosentino](https://www.cosentino.com/)

**Apply to Hommet:** Lead with the integrated company proposition, then provide a clear category index. Use partner identities as supporting evidence within Hommet's experience. Let real projects connect products to applications. Keep detailed professional information accessible without overwhelming the homepage.

**Boundary:** Cosentino is a design reference, not a Hommet partner. Its products, imagery and claims must not appear as Hommet offerings. Do not reproduce its large catalogue or global navigation.

#### Kingspan — distinct product worlds

**Observed:** The root site opens with country selection. The group site separates company, investor and careers information, and identifies projects by different divisions, including insulation, panels, roofing and data solutions. [Sources: country gateway](https://www.kingspan.com/), [group site](https://www.kingspan.com/en)

**Apply to Hommet:** Give Armstrong a named technical destination with a clear return to Hommet. Maintain shared wayfinding while allowing a distinct visual identity and enquiry contact. Keep corporate credibility in About and Projects.

**Boundary:** Hommet does not need a global country selector or a public-company investor centre. The reference does not establish equivalent scale or credentials for Hommet.

#### Silestone — brand identity within a parent

**Observed:** `silestone.com` redirects to Cosentino's Silestone section. It retains the parent navigation while presenting a dedicated brand introduction, applications and technical explanation. [Source: Silestone](https://www.cosentino.com/silestone/)

**Apply to Hommet:** Give Atlantique a recognisable introduction and a deliberate onward journey. Preserve the Hommet context before visitors leave for roofing detail.

**Boundary:** This reference currently lives under its parent; it is not evidence that Hommet should bring Atlantique's catalogue onto its own domain. The brief explicitly requires an external handoff.

#### Dekton — layered product information

**Observed:** `dekton.com` redirects to Cosentino's Dekton section. The page progresses through product explanation, benefits, design options and technical detail. [Source: Dekton](https://www.cosentino.com/dekton/)

**Apply to Hommet:** Let technical categories reveal information progressively: purpose, applications, approved specifications, available documents, then a relevant enquiry route. Use detailed sections only when real product information exists.

**Boundary:** Do not transfer Dekton's performance claims, certifications or specifications to Hommet products. Consumer category pages remain Hommet pages rather than individual partner storefronts.

#### IMKAT — selective inspiration

**Observed:** The retrieved homepage includes project imagery, model listings, consultation steps, contact actions, pricing tools and promotional content. It is more sales-oriented and extensive than the brief's shorthand description of a restrained mood reference. Image authenticity was not independently verified. [Source: IMKAT](https://imkatconstruction.com/)

**Apply to Hommet:** Take the useful connection between completed work, practical information and an enquiry. Pair genuine Hommet photography with concise captions and a clear next step.

**Boundary:** Do not import its promotions, cost calculator, claims, repeated sales language or single-service structure. The photography restraint is a Hommet requirement, not an assertion that every reference image meets it.

#### Turn Media article — supporting editorial guidance

**Observed:** The article advocates clear positioning, real projects, useful product detail, contextual calls to action and usable mobile journeys. It also recommends broader content and location pages. Its conversion and search-ranking assertions are the author's claims, not independently validated findings here. [Source: construction website article](https://turnxmedia.com/blog/the-best-construction-website)

**Apply to Hommet:** Keep each category useful, test enquiry journeys on phones, and place downloads or technical consultation actions where relevant.

**Boundary:** Do not add a blog, calculators or mass-produced city pages to the agreed scope. Do not reproduce article statistics as Hommet evidence or promise search results.

Use a light editorial layout: warm ivory surfaces, generous spacing, strong green headings and restrained image groupings. Avoid decorative animation, autoplay video and a rotating hero. These are proposed layout choices to support the brief's calm tone and load-time requirement.

- Accessible interaction: labelled fields, visible keyboard focus, meaningful image alternatives, comfortable touch targets, keyboard-operable menus and reduced-motion support.

### 7. Assets, performance and scalable publishing

**Proposed asset request:** original real photographs at least 2000px on the long edge where available; both landscape and portrait compositions; image captions, location, Hommet's role and usage permission; approved logo vectors or transparent high-resolution files; current product data and dated PDF datasheets. These are requested source assets, not page download sizes. The extracted DOCX images are reference material, not a complete launch asset set.

**Performance plan:** serve responsive compressed images, prioritise the hero and lazy-load below-fold media; use a small self-hosted Manrope font set; defer maps and optional scripts; avoid heavy video and animation; cache static assets and keep page scripts small. Suggested starting budgets are a roughly 200KB mobile hero and under 1MB initial homepage transfer, to be validated with actual assets.

Record visible-content timing, largest-content timing, total load timing and Lighthouse/PageSpeed reports. The brief's under-three-second target is an acceptance requirement, not a result established by this Markdown document. Clarify its exact load metric before sign-off; do not substitute a score alone for timed testing.

**Growth:** maintain reusable category, product, partner, project, document, office and contact records. Let page depth vary without new templates for every partner. Keep general and Armstrong contacts separate. Store draft/approved status so missing data and unapproved statistics cannot appear publicly.

### 1.  Who Hommet is, and what the site must say

Positioning:  Integrated Building & Living Solutions. Hommet is the single building partner people trust for their home, from foundation to roof, backed by internationally trusted brands.

From foundation to roof, backed by trusted brands.  This is the line that captures Hommet. The site should make a visitor feel it covers the whole home, not one product. Roofing is the flagship that leads, but the brand is the umbrella over every category.

Geographic claim:  Rooted in Kerala, earning trust across India. Hommet delivers across India, so this is the honest present-tense claim. Do not shrink it to Kerala-only, and do not stretch it to global.

#### Message hierarchy for the homepage

- Tagline:  Crafting Durable Dreams

- Headline:  Everything your home needs, from one partner you trust

- Proof line:  Rooted in Kerala. Earning trust across India.

- Supporting line:  From foundation to roof, backed by internationally trusted brands, and known for getting it right.

Tone of the whole site:  warm but firm. Calm, credible, handled. We do not argue our quality, we radiate it. No hype, no exclamation marks, no wellness or salesy jargon.

Look and feel:  warm premium. Clean, spacious, considered, but warm, not cold or corporate. This is the feel to convey all through the site: generous whitespace, calm typography, the olive-and-cream palette, restraint over decoration.

Do not use the word "premium" in the copy.  Premium is a feeling the design carries, never a claim we make. The website copy should not say "premium" (or "luxury," "world-class," or similar self-praise) anywhere. We show it through quality and restraint; we do not announce it. The word is for this brief and the design team, not for the visitor.

Primary audience:  builders and contractors first. The site serves a wide audience (homeowners, architects, engineers, developers, dealers, consultants, facility managers, institutions), but when a choice has to be made about tone or hierarchy, speak to builders and contractors who buy on trust, spec, and reliability.

First ten seconds:  a visitor should feel, within ten seconds, that Hommet is trusted, considered, and integrated, a partner for the whole home, not a single-product seller. That impression comes from the design and photography, not from us saying it.

### 2.  What the site is for

The website carries more than product awareness. Design and structure should serve these objectives, roughly in priority order.

- Corporate brand positioning.  Establish Hommet as a credible, premium, integrated building solutions company, not a single-product seller.

- Lead generation.  Commercial project enquiries, residential enquiries, and consultation requests are the core conversions.

- Product awareness.  Show the full range clearly so visitors grasp the breadth.

- Dealer acquisition.  A clear path for prospective dealers and partners to reach us.

- Architect and consultant engagement.  Make it easy for specifiers to find technical detail and enquire.

- Corporate and investor credibility.  The About and Projects sections should read as a company with standing, not a catalogue.

- Recruitment.  A simple careers path.

### 4.  Typography

- Body:  Manrope Regular / Medium, highly readable at small sizes on mobile. Generous line spacing.

- Armstrong section only:  its own technical stack (e.g. Space Grotesk display, Inter body, a mono face for specs). Keep it visually walled off from the consumer pages.

- Text colour:  body text is a soft warm grey (around #4A4A47), not pure black. It reads calmer and more premium against the ivory background. Headings use the brand greens (Deep Forest #263A1B, Olive #687C3C). Reserve near-black for nothing; the grey and greens carry everything.
